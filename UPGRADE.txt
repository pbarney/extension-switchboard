Extension Switchboard v0.7.0

Added manual category ordering by drag and drop.

Changes:
- User-created categories now show a dedicated drag handle.
- Dragging above or below another user category changes its saved position.
- All extensions and Uncategorized remain fixed at the top.
- Drop-position indicators show where a category will be inserted.
- The category list auto-scrolls when dragging near its top or bottom edge.
- Category order saves immediately and is reflected in extension category dropdowns.
- Import/export retains category order through the existing categories array.
- No configuration schema migration is required.

Upgrade:
Replace ExtensionSwitchboard.uc.js with the v0.7.0 script and restart Firefox completely.
